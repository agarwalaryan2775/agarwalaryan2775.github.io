Privacy Policy

8D Sukoon Publisher. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who uses this app

8D Sukoon Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the 8D Sukoon and valowaliii channels to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's own sign-in page, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's basic details (to check it is the right channel). It does not read, collect or share anyone else's data.

What it stores

The sign-in key Google returns is stored only on the owner's own computer, so uploads can continue without signing in each time. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. YouTube content is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
